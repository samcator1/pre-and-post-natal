# Pre- and Post-Natal Family Playbook 👶

Welcome to our family preparation hub for the arrival of our first child. This repository holds our complete, practical planning documents—tailored to our preferences and the **Norfolk and Norwich University Hospital (NNUH)** / **NHS** framework.

Each document is modular, practical, and designed to be easily referenced on a phone or laptop.

## ⚡ "In Labour" — Partner 3-Step Immediate Action Plan

1. **Assess & Time:**
   - Note contraction frequency, duration, and intensity (use a contraction timer app).
   - Check mum’s comfort (dim lights, cue playlist, offer sips of water/isotonic drink, setup TENS machine).
2. **Call NNUH Maternity Triage:**
   - Call **01603 287328** when contractions are **3 in 10 minutes** lasting **60 seconds**, or immediately if waters break or fluid is green/brown.
   - Have mum's **NHS number / Badger Notes app** open and ready.
3. **Grab & Go:**
   - Grab the **3 Hospital Bags** from by the front door.
   - Confirm car fuel/charge and install infant car seat base.
   - Navigate to NNUH (West Outpatients entrance / Maternity entrance drop-off zone).
