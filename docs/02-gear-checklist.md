# 02. Gear & Acquisition Checklist 🛒

A priority-tiered, zone-by-zone inventory to ensure we have exactly what we need—without cluttering the house with unnecessary gadgets.

### Status Legend
- `[Day 1 Essential]` — Must be in the house, assembled, and ready before baby arrives.
- `[Weeks 1–4]` — Needed shortly after coming home; can be acquired or unboxed during month one.
- `[Can Wait (Months 2–3+)]` — Non-urgent; safe to hold off until baby’s personality and routines develop.
- Checkboxes: `[x]` Acquired (Bought / Borrowed / Gifted) | `[ ]` Still Needed

---
