## 🏛️ Norfolk Birth Registration Guide (Within 42 Days)

In England and Wales, you are legally required to register your baby's birth within **42 days**.

### 1. Where to Register in Norfolk
- **Booking Portal:** [Norfolk County Council - Register a Birth](https://www.norfolk.gov.uk/births-ceremonies-and-deaths/births/register-a-birth)
- **Norwich Office:**  
  *The Archive Centre, County Hall, Martineau Lane, Norwich, NR1 2DQ*  
  *(Alternative offices available in Great Yarmouth, King's Lynn, Dereham, etc.)*

### 2. Who Must Attend?
- **If married or in a civil partnership:** Either parent can attend alone to register.
- **If unmarried:** Both parents must attend together in person for the father/second parent's details to be included on the birth certificate.

### 3. What to Bring to the Appointment
- [ ] Baby’s red book (PCHR) or hospital discharge paper showing NHS number.
- [ ] Parents' passports or driving licenses (proof of identity).
- [ ] Proof of address (utility bill or council tax statement).
- [ ] Marriage / civil partnership certificate (if applicable).
- [ ] Debit/credit card to pay for birth certificates (£12.50 each).  
  *Tip: Buy at least **2–3 copies** of the **full certificate** (showing parents' names), as they are required for passport applications, school enrollments, and child trust accounts.*

## 💷 HMRC Child Benefit & Financial Setup

### 1. Claiming Child Benefit Online
- **When to Apply:** As soon as the birth is registered and you have the birth certificate reference number.
- **How to Apply:** Apply directly via your [HMRC Government Gateway account](https://www.gov.uk/child-benefit/how-to-claim) or the HMRC app. Claims can be backdated up to 3 months.
- **Why It Matters (Even if High Earner):** If one parent earns over £60,000, you may be subject to the High Income Child Benefit Charge. However, **still fill out the claim form and choose not to receive payments** to ensure the non-working/lower-earning parent gets **National Insurance credits** towards their State Pension and baby gets their National Insurance number automatically at age 16!

### 2. Junior ISA (JISA) Setup
- Once baby has an official birth certificate, you can open a tax-free Junior Stocks & Shares ISA (e.g. Vanguard, Fidelity, Hargreaves Lansdown) allowing family and grandparents to gift money directly towards their 18th birthday.

### ⚠️ Crucial: 8-Week MenB Infant Paracetamol Protocol
The Meningococcal B (MenB) vaccine causes a high fever in over 50% of babies if paracetamol is not given. The NHS explicitly recommends **3 doses of Infant Paracetamol (Calpol 120mg/5ml)**:

- **Dose 1 (2.5ml):** Given by parents **as soon as possible after the injection** (take Calpol and syringe with you to the GP clinic).
- **Dose 2 (2.5ml):** Given **4 to 6 hours** after the first dose.
- **Dose 3 (2.5ml):** Given **4 to 6 hours** after the second dose.

*Keep Calpol Infant and the measuring syringe ready at home prior to the 8-week mark!*
